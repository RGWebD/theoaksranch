From Start to Finish

Picture your day.

Having your rehearsal, ceremony and reception in one place keeps every guest with you, from the first look to the final send-off.

  1. Morning

    Getting ready

    Settle into the a/c bridal suite and groom's room, each with private restrooms, then gather your people beneath the oaks.

  2. Midday

    Setting the scene

    Rustic seating waits at the ceremony site, with curtains, shepherd hooks and signs on hand for your finishing touches.

  3. Afternoon

    “I do”

    Walk the aisle at the ceremony site you love most, framed in white drapes and lanterns.

  4. Golden Hour

    Portraits under the oaks

    100 acres of moss-draped oaks and open lawn make every portrait feel like a painting.

  5. Evening

    Dinner & dancing

    Toast in the barn, then dance beneath the string lights on the stable area's lighted dance floor.
