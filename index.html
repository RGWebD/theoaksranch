Welcome to The Oaks

Shady oaks, Spanish moss & quiet country.

The Oaks Ranch is 100 acres of shady oak trees draped in Spanish moss and miles of serene trails in Florida back country. It is a beautiful, secluded setting to have your wedding and reception.

Nestled in the quiet country, The Oaks Ranch is where nature and a sense of tranquility come together, on a gated, secure property that welcomes very large groups as well as small, intimate ones.
