Before You Visit

Good to know.

Don't see yours? Call (904) 284‑1793 or email theoaksranchbarnweddings@gmail.com.

Where is The Oaks Ranch?

We're at 5779 Hogarth Rd, Green Cove Springs, FL 32043, in the quiet country of Clay County. Get directions →

How big is the property?

The Oaks Ranch is a gated and secure 100-acre facility of shady oak trees draped in Spanish moss, with miles of serene trails in Florida back country.

How many ceremony sites are there?

Three separate ceremony sites, each with rustic seating, including the White Colonnades and the Black Pergola. There are curtains at the ceremony sites, the stable area and the main barn.

Is there an indoor space?

Yes. Our 4,000 sq. ft. barn has a/c and heat, and the stable area has a lighted dance floor for rehearsal, dining and receptions.

Where do the bride and groom get ready?

Every wedding includes an a/c bridal suite and groom's room for getting ready, each with private restrooms.

How many guests can you host?

We can accommodate very large groups, up to 300 guests, as well as small, intimate groups.

What do wedding packages cost?

We specialize in a 2-day wedding and reception package for $4,000. We also have a 1-day wedding and reception package for $3,000, with the same amenities. Please confirm current pricing and availability when you inquire.

Are tables and chairs included?

There are 26 six-ft round tables and 4 nine-ft farmhouse tables you're welcome to use. We do not have chairs, so plan to rent those.

What decor is available to use?

Spools, shepherd hooks, a whiskey barrel bar, rustic cake stands, signs and various other items, plus curtains and decorative lighting throughout.

Can we have our rehearsal, ceremony and reception in one place?

Yes. The stable area's lighted dance floor works for your rehearsal, dining and reception, and the ceremony sites are right on the property.

Is the venue accessible?

We have 3 ADA-approved bathrooms on site.

How do I schedule a tour?

Call (904) 284-1793, email us to schedule a tour, or use the inquiry form.
