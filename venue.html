The Venue

Every corner, shaded by the oaks

Three ceremony sites, a 4,000 sq. ft. barn with a/c and heat, the stable dance floor and 100 acres of Florida back country.

Schedule a TourContact Us
01

The Barn

A 4,000 sq. ft. barn with a/c and heat, dressed with curtains and decorative lighting, so the weather never decides your day. Bring your vision and make it yours.

  • 4,000 sq. ft. with a/c & heat
  • 3 ADA-approved bathrooms
  • A/C bridal suite & groom's room with private restrooms
02

Three Ceremony Sites

Choose from three separate ceremony sites with rustic seating, from the White Colonnades with their chapel doors to the Black Pergola garden, each dressed in curtains and shaded by moss-hung oaks.

  • Three separate ceremony sites
  • Rustic seating at each site
  • Curtains & decorative lighting
03

The Stable Area

The stable area has a lighted dance floor for your rehearsal, dining and reception. When the sun goes down, the string lights come up.

  • Lighted dance floor
  • Rehearsal, dining & receptions
  • Curtains & string lights
04

The Grounds

100 acres of shady oak trees draped in Spanish moss, miles of serene trails and a quiet lake, all behind a gated, secure entrance in Florida back country.

  • 100 gated, secure acres
  • Miles of serene trails
  • Moss-draped oaks for portraits
